# PROJECT DEFINITION

## 1. One-line definition
A mobile-first restaurant ordering + operations system for Naoki Chicken Parangtritis, designed to become the outlet's practical replacement for Olsera.

## 2. Primary users
1. Owner / outlet partner — full control.
2. Admin — daily management.
3. Cashier — POS and payment handling.
4. Kitchen — order preparation and status updates.
5. Customer — browse/order/pay/track.

## 3. Core success condition
A normal business day can be operated without relying on the legacy POS for the workflows migrated to the new system.

## 4. Success metrics for MVP
- Customer can create an order successfully.
- Staff can receive and process it.
- Cashier can complete the transaction.
- Kitchen can see the correct items and update status.
- Stock availability blocks unavailable products.
- Owner can reconcile sales and cash.
- Staff permissions prevent unrelated access.
- The app remains usable on a normal Android phone.

## 5. Product principle
**Simple where the outlet is simple; deep where the business needs control.**
