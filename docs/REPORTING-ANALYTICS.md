# REPORTING & ANALYTICS

## Required reports
### Sales
- gross sales
- discounts
- net sales
- completed orders
- average order value

### Orders
- by channel
- by hour/day
- status distribution

### Product
- units sold
- revenue contribution
- unavailable periods

### Finance
- cash collected
- non-cash payment totals
- expenses
- cash session variance

## Dashboard cards
Keep cards tied to real queries. Every metric documents:
- source table(s)
- filter/window
- calculation
- timezone

## Date handling
Business reporting uses Asia/Jakarta display boundaries, while storage may remain UTC.
